# OpenShore Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains how you pay for OpenShore, how renewal works, how to
cancel, and when you get your money back. Plain language, because a refund
policy you cannot follow is no policy at all.

## 1. During the beta

During the beta, nothing is charged for the agent. That includes the iPhone
beta on TestFlight and the desktop apps.

If you are ever charged for the agent during the beta, write to us and we will
refund it.

Charges from a cloud model provider on your own key are not ours. Your provider
bills you for them under its own terms, and refunds, if any, come from it.

## 2. The plans

After the beta there are two ways to pay:

- **Personal.** An annual subscription for one person, bought in the iPhone
  app through Apple.
- **Team plans.** Annual subscriptions for a team, bought on the web through
  Stripe. The iPhone and iPad app shows seat counts only. It has no way to buy
  them.

You pay the price shown at checkout. Tax is shown at checkout where it applies.

Team plans are not yet sold to customers in the European Union or the United
Kingdom. See section 7.

## 3. Personal: bought through Apple

Apple sells, bills, and renews the Personal plan. Apple's terms apply to the
purchase, alongside ours.

**Renewal.** Your subscription renews automatically every year, at the price
shown at purchase, until you cancel. Apple charges your Apple account when it
renews. It renews unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the
end of the current year.

**How to cancel.** On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, tap
Subscriptions, then tap OpenShore. You keep access until the end of the year you
paid for.

Deleting your OpenShore account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it
in Settings as well.

**Refunds.** Apple decides refunds for purchases made through Apple. Ask at
reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue or block a refund for an Apple
purchase.

**Price changes.** Apple tells you about a price change before it applies,
under Apple's rules, and asks for your agreement where those rules require it.

## 4. Team plans: bought through Stripe

**Renewal.** A team plan renews automatically every year until it is
cancelled. Stripe charges the card on file at renewal.

**Renewal reminder.** We email the team's billing contact 30 days before each
annual renewal, with the date and the amount.

**Price changes.** A new price applies only at renewal, never in the middle of a
paid year. We tell you at least 30 days before the renewal it applies to, so you
have time to cancel.

**Refunds within 14 days.** You get a full refund if you ask within 14 days of
the first charge. The same applies to every renewal: a full refund if you ask
within 14 days of that renewal charge. A refund ends the plan, and the team's
paid features stop when the refund is issued.

**After 14 days.** You can still cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect
at the end of the year you paid for, and the team keeps access until then. There
is no refund for the unused part of the year.

**Deleting a team.** Deleting a team cancels its Stripe subscription. Before it
does, the flow offers "Cancel renewal instead," which keeps the team until the
end of the paid year. If you delete the team within 14 days of a charge, you get
a full refund of that charge. After 14 days, deleting the team ends access at
once, and there is no refund for the unused part of the year. If you want to
keep using the time you paid for, choose "Cancel renewal instead."

## 5. How to ask for a refund

**Personal (Apple).** At reportaproblem.apple.com.

**Team plans (Stripe).** Write to os-code@openshorellc.com from the team's
billing email, with the team name and the date of the charge. Stripe returns
the money to the card you paid with. Your bank decides how long it takes to show
up.

## 6. Chargebacks

If something looks wrong on your statement, please write to us first. Most
problems are faster to fix directly than through a bank dispute.

You keep every right your bank and card network give you. If you open a
chargeback on a team plan, we may pause the team's paid features while the
dispute is open, and we will answer the bank with the record of the charge.

## 7. The European Union and the United Kingdom

Team plans are not yet offered to customers in the European Union or the
United Kingdom. They will be once counsel has ruled on the representatives the
law requires there.

When they are offered, the following applies to consumers, meaning people
buying for themselves and not for a business:

- Consumers in the EU and the UK have a legal right to withdraw from an online
  purchase within 14 days, without giving a reason. Our 14-day full refund in
  section 4 is meant to meet or go beyond that right, and it does not replace
  it.
- Nothing in this policy takes away a right your local consumer law gives you.

For the Personal plan bought through Apple, Apple handles withdrawal requests
at reportaproblem.apple.com.
