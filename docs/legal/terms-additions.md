(`docs/terms-of-use.md`), for counsel to edit. The existing sections 1 to 8
stand as written. The existing section 9 (Changes) and section 10 (Contact) are

- After existing section 1: A3 (accounts), A4 (beta), A5 (paying).

Apple bills it, renews it, and handles refunds. Apple's terms apply to the
purchase as well as ours.

**Team plans** are annual subscriptions bought on the web through Stripe.

For both:

- **You pay the price shown at checkout**, plus any tax shown there.
- **Plans renew automatically every year** until you cancel. By buying a plan,
  you authorize the renewal charge each year at the then-current price.
- **For team plans**, we email a reminder 30 days before each renewal. A new
  price applies only at renewal, and we tell you at least 30 days before. You
  can cancel online at any time through the Stripe Customer Portal.
- **For Personal**, cancel in iOS Settings under Subscriptions.

full refund within 14 days of each annual charge, and after that, cancellation
