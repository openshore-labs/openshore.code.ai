# OpenShore Accessibility Statement

DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW. Not in effect. Prepared 2026-09-24.

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

OpenShore should work for everyone who builds software, however they see,
hear, move, or read. This statement covers the website openshore.ai, the
desktop apps for Linux, macOS, and Windows, and the iPhone and iPad app.

## 1. Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA
on the website and in the apps.

This is our goal, not a claim that we already meet it everywhere. Section 3
lists what we know falls short.

## 2. What we have done

- **Contrast.** We fixed text and control colors that did not meet the WCAG
  contrast ratios.
- **Dialogs and sheets.** When a dialog opens, focus moves into it and stays
  there until it closes, then returns to where you were.
- **Labels.** Buttons, fields, and icons carry names that screen readers can
  announce.
- **Keyboard.** You can reach and use controls with a keyboard.
- **Reduced motion.** If your system asks for reduced motion, the site and the
  apps turn their animations off.
- **Zoom.** Pinch zoom works on the site. We do not block it.
