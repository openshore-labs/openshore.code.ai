# OpenShore strategy docs

Positioning and product-strategy references for OpenShore (OS Code). Each is a
self-contained HTML page (openshore.ai brand, works offline, light and dark).
Open any file in a browser. These are internal reference material, not part of
the app build.

## Contents

- **`competitive-landscape.html`** — an evidence brief mapping OpenShore against
  its rivals on two axes, Sovereignty (rented cloud to owned, local, open,
  private) and Reach (single surface to genuinely cross-device). Plots the four
  established neighborhoods (cloud coding agents, local model runners, on-device
  mobile, open-source self-hosted assistants) plus the emerging 2026 wave that is
  now racing for the same corner (OpenMono, Paseo). Claims are graded
  Benchmarked / Assumed / Measured, with an honest note that the corner is
  contested, not empty. Competitor facts verified against primary sources,
  August 2026.

- **`product-deep-dive.html`** — everything built in the app today, and what each
  piece becomes at full strength, sequenced along the soup-to-nuts build loop
  (idea, local models, the agent, owned storage, cross-device, ship). Every
  capability is graded Shipped / Partial / At full strength. Built from a
  code-level inventory of both `app/` and `os-code/`.

- **`executive-brief.html`** — a one-screen summary of the above for sharing: the
  loop, what makes it different, the moat, pricing, and where the product stands.

## The through-line

OpenShore is a way to build an app soup to nuts on your own machines, private the
whole way. The competitive point and the product point are the same one: rivals
own a single slice of that loop, OpenShore owns the whole loop, and the moat is
the closed loop executed to product finish, not "local AI" as an idea.

## Keeping them current

This is a fast-moving field. Re-check any single competitor claim before acting
on it, and refresh the deep dive when a Partial item flips to Shipped. The pages
were authored to match the openshore.ai brand tokens (Fraunces and Inter, cream
paper and deep ink, teal for local and amber for cloud) and carry no em dashes,
in line with the repo policy.
